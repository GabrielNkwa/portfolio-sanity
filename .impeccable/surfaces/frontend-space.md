# Surface brief: frontend_space home page

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a startup founder or CTO, evening, laptop, comparing a few engineers and deciding in minutes whether to book an intro call. Action: "Book an intro call" (scroll to the contact form). Proof: real Sanity projects and screenshots only. Constraints: no invented metrics, quotes or logos; process copy is a draft for Gabriel to confirm. Unresolved: calendar link, extra form fields, case-study pages (see docs/redesign-plan.md).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one flight. A single starfield stays behind every section and answers scroll speed, so the visitor feels progress through the page. It refuses the agency-portfolio default of a giant name over a screenshot mosaic.

OWN-WORLD: Pure black ground, near-white text, one amber accent (sunlight on a planet's limb) used for the primary action, the horizon glow and the timeline. Geist semibold with tight tracking, sentence case. 16px panels with a single hairline border, no shadows on panels, no grain, no custom cursor. A CSS planet horizon with a lit edge bookends the hero and the contact section.

STORY: The visitor understands in one viewport that one engineer builds and launches web and mobile products, sees real shipped products at full size, learns how an engagement runs, and books an intro call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed nav at the top with brand left and "Book an intro call" right. Headline "Web and mobile products, built and launched." left-aligned, about 90px, in the upper-middle. Under it one sentence of positioning, the amber primary button and a text link to the work, then a line naming Pharma-AID Africa, Valdin Energy and Fantasy Pro League. The planet horizon fills the bottom third with its amber limb; stars drift behind everything.

FORM: Pinned by the brief (black Apple-style system plus space travel), not rolled; the dealt roll was seed:30f2c50faaf6e6d0 and was set aside under "a user- or brief-pinned decision beats the roll". Signature interaction: starfield warp driven by scroll velocity, with the planet horizon dropping away and returning at the contact section.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
