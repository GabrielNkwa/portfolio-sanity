## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing: Vite, React 18, CSS Modules, framer-motion, Sanity (read-only CDN query) and a Vercel function at `api/contact.js`. The Apollo redesign replaced the earlier Volt design in `frontend_react/`, keeping the same data layer and contact endpoint.

## Users
Startup founders and CTOs who need one senior engineer to own a web or mobile product from first spec to production. They are comparing a few engineers, have a deadline, and decide from the work shown and how clearly the offer is explained.

## Product Purpose
Personal site for Gabriel Nkwa, project engineer in Abuja. It converts a founder or CTO into a scoped intro call for fixed-scope project delivery (brief, build, launch). Success is a qualified message through the contact form.

## Positioning
One engineer who designs, builds and launches both web platforms and native mobile apps, so a small team gets one accountable owner instead of coordinating several people. Shipped work includes Pharma-AID Africa, Valdin Energy and Fantasy Pro League.

## Operating Context
Content comes from Sanity (`works`, `abouts`, `experiences`, `skills`). Contact messages are stored in Sanity through `/api/contact`. The site is deployed on Vercel at gabriel-nkwa.vercel.app.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Gabriel Nkwa", title "Project Engineer", Abuja. Plain, direct voice. Space-travel theme requested for the redesign, with a black Apple-style system.

## Evidence on Hand
Project names, descriptions, tags, screenshots and links in Sanity; role history by year in `experiences`; a skills list. There are no testimonials, client logos, metrics or pricing, and none may be invented. Result claims stay out until Gabriel supplies real ones.

## Product Principles
1. Lead with what a founder gets (a launched product), not with the engineer's tools.
2. Every claim on the page traces to CMS content or to Gabriel.
3. One clear action: book a scoped intro call.
4. The work is shown at full size and readable before any effect competes with it.
5. Motion explains progress through the page and never blocks reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keep the current standard: skip link, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, AA contrast, content readable without JavaScript effects.
